## A. 운동

### A-1. 혈당이 내려가는 방향 (1형)

ISPAD 2022 운동, 5 PHYSIOLOGY:
> Aerobic exercise tends to cause circulating glucose levels to drop, while anaerobic or mixed forms of exercise are typically associated with an attenuated drop or a rise in glycemia. In general, mixed activities tend to have a moderating effect.

ISPAD 2022 운동, 5.3 Mixed exercise:
> Most forms of PA for many youth consist of spontaneous play and/or team and field sports.

> This type of "interval" or "mixed" activity has been shown to result in a lesser rate of fall glycemia in persons with T1D compared to continuous moderate‐intensity exercise, both during and after the event.

ISPAD 2022 운동, 5.2 Very high intensity and anaerobic exercise:
> Anaerobic activities like sprinting, and weightlifting can cause glucose levels to rise, particularly if done early in the day with little to no prandial insulin in the circulation and if the activity is performed in isolation

- 이 장 스스로 근거 대부분이 성인 1형 연구라고 적었다(2절 끝: "many of the recommendations in this guideline are based on data derived from studies in adults with T1D").

### A-2. 운동 중·뒤 저혈당 위험 (1형)

ISPAD 2022 운동, 2 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY AND RECOMMENDATIONS:
> There is an increased risk of hypoglycemia during, shortly after, and up to 24 h after exercise due to increased insulin sensitivity. A

> During all forms of physical exercise high glycemic index carbohydrates should be available to prevent and treat hypoglycemia. E

ISPAD 2022 저혈당, 2 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY AND RECOMMENDATIONS:
> Glucose monitoring should be performed prior to exercise, and extra carbohydrates may be consumed based on the glucose level and the expected intensity and duration of exercise. B

### A-3. 짧은 놀이 (1형)

ISPAD 2022 운동, 7.7 Unplanned exercise:
> Unplanned opportunistic activities such as jumping on a trampoline or playing at school break (recess) time usually last less than 15 min and rarely cause hypoglycemia. However, if these activities last longer than 15 min, rapidly absorbed carbohydrates will likely be required.

### A-4. 체육 시간·저녁 운동과 밤 (1형)

ISPAD 2022 운동, 7.5 Overnight following planned activity:
> Following exercise lasting 45 min the risk of hypoglycemia lasts for 7–11 h, which increases the risk of overnight hypoglycemia for activity performed after 4 p.m.

> Exercise for 45 min performed at midday does not have the same hypoglycemia‐inducing effect overnight and therefore does not require the same adjustments. This is important for school‐aged children as it suggests that basal insulin dose adjustment is not required following daytime sports classes or lunchtime activities.

ISPAD 2022 운동, 11 SCHOOLS AND CAMPS:
> Students with diabetes should fully participate in physical education classes and other physical activities at school provided they do have any contraindications to exercise.

> For physical education lessons, a diabetes care plan should be developed, including detailed instructions for the students and their teachers and coaches. The main goal is to avoid hypoglycemia during and after exercise.

### A-5. 2형 아동

ISPAD 2022 운동, 5.4 Reasons for dysglycemia during exercise in youth with T1D:
> For youth with T2D, there is little evidence for the influence of duration, type, or intensity of exercise on acute glycemic excursions or glucose time‐in‐range.

ISPAD 2022 운동, 5 PHYSIOLOGY:
> The acute effects of anaerobic exercise on glycemia for youth with T2D are unclear.

ISPAD 2022 운동, 2 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY AND RECOMMENDATIONS:
> Children and adolescents with T1D and T2D should be encouraged and supported to achieve the recommended 60 min of moderate to vigorous intensity PA every day. B

ISPAD 2022 운동, 3 INTRODUCTION:
> Children and adolescents should limit the amount of time spent being sedentary, particularly the amount of recreational screen time.

## B. 음식

### B-1. 단 음료

ISPAD 2022 영양, 5.1 Carbohydrates (PDF 5쪽):
> Large quantities of sugary beverages cause high postprandial glucose peaks that are difficult to adequately cover with insulin. The consumption of sweetened drinks, soft drinks and cordials should be discouraged for the whole family.

ISPAD 2022 영양, 2형 영양 관리 부분 (PDF 15쪽):
> Simply eliminating sugary beverages such as soft drinks and fruit juices can accomplish improvement in blood glucose and weight.

### B-2. 기름지고 단백질 많은 식사 (외식)

ISPAD 2022 영양, 10.1 Fat and protein (PDF 11쪽):
> Observations from pediatric and adult studies have shown that meals high in either protein or fat increase delayed hyperglycemia (up to 3–6 h after the meal) and reduce the early (1–2 h) postprandial rise.

### B-3. 잔치·특별한 날

ISPAD 2022 영양, 12 FESTIVITIES AND SPECIAL EVENTS (PDF 13쪽):
> Emphasis needs to be placed on the importance of routine with respect to meal timings rather than following an erratic and frequent eating pattern

> A nutritional assessment reviewing carbohydrate intake with guidance on making healthy food choices, moderation, portion control, reading nutrition labels, maintaining appropriate energy, adequate hydration and physical activity should be given.

ISPAD 2022 영양, 11.1 (학령기, PDF 13쪽):
> when arranging playdates, sleepovers and parties, families are encouraged to discuss their child's normal routine for food, physical activity and sleep with other family members and friends, and be available to support their child's diabetes management.

## C. 수면

### C-1. 1형: 잠과 저혈당 (소아 대상 장)

ISPAD 2022 저혈당, 2 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY AND RECOMMENDATIONS:
> Common precipitants for hypoglycemia include excessive insulin dose, missed meals, exercise, sleep, and in adolescents, alcohol ingestion. Risk factors include previous severe hypoglycemic events and reduced hypoglycemia awareness. B

> Glucose levels are recommended to be monitored overnight particularly if there is an additional risk factor that may predispose to nocturnal hypoglycemia. E

ISPAD 2022 저혈당, Table 3 아래 Nocturnal hypoglycemia 부분:
> The counterregulatory responses to hypoglycemia are attenuated during sleep

> Younger age, lower HbA1c levels, antecedent exercise, and hypoglycemia are associated with a greater frequency of nocturnal hypoglycemia.

### C-2. 두 유형: 수면 습관 (ADA, 소아 전용 아님, 원문 대조 전)

ADA 2026 5장 Sleep Health, 권고 5.57: 수면을 돕는 일과·습관을 상담한다. 본문에 수면 장애가 자기 관리 참여를 줄이고 혈당을 목표 범위에 두는 것을 방해할 수 있다는 문장이 있다. 글자 그대로의 인용은 원문 대조 뒤에 적는다.

## D. 2-B 설계에 주는 뜻 (해석, 결정 아님)

| 맵 | 1형 | 2형 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 운동장·아침 | 운동 중 하강은 근거 있음(A-1, A-2). 운동 전 혈당 재기 습관도 근거 있음(A-2 저혈당 장). 단거리 질주처럼 아주 센 운동은 오를 수도 있어(A-1) "운동 = 언제나 내려감"으로 가르치면 틀린다 | 운동 직후 혈당 변화는 소아 근거가 적다(A-5). 값으로 혈당을 바꾸기보다 "매일 움직이기, 오래 앉아 있지 않기" 습관으로 다루는 쪽이 근거에 맞다 |
| 놀이터·오후 | 놀이는 섞인 운동이라 하강이 덜하고, 15분 안쪽 놀이는 저혈당을 드물게 일으킨다(A-1, A-3). 후보였던 "급하강 확률 올리기"는 근거가 약하다 | 쉬기와 움직이기 균형은 A-5의 "앉아 있는 시간 줄이기"와 맞는다 |
| 나들이·저녁 | 외식 음식은 나중에 오르는 쪽(B-2)이지만 "1형 일반 음식 처리"가 정해지지 않아 막혀 있다. 저녁 운동 뒤 밤 저혈당 위험(A-4)은 집·밤과 이어 쓸 수 있다 | 단 음료를 물로 바꾸기, 양 조절(B-1, B-3). "나쁜 음식" 금지 규칙 안에서 다뤄야 한다 |
| 집·밤 | 잠은 저혈당 계기다(C-1). "자기 전 혈당 재기" 습관 순간은 근거 있음 | 혈당 영향 값 근거 없음. 수면 습관 장면만(C-2) |
